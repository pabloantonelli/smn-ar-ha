DejaVuSans.ttf — DejaVu Fonts, based on Bitstream Vera Fonts.
Bundled here so the radar camera's alert caption can render Spanish
accented characters (á, é, í, ó, ú, ñ) regardless of what fonts are
available inside the Home Assistant environment (Pillow's own
ImageFont.load_default() doesn't cover them).
